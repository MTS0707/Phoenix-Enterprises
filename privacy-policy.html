Last updated: 27 July 2026

Phoenix Enterprises ("we", "us", "our") operates this website to share information about our cutting-tool products, services, and business. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit or use this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information you provide to us

When you fill in the contact form on our Contact page, we collect the details you enter: name, company name, email address, phone number, and your message. This form currently opens your own email client with those details pre-filled, addressed to info@phoenixenter.com — the information is sent from your device, not stored on this website.

If you call or email us directly using the phone numbers or email address listed on this site, that communication is subject to the privacy practices of the service you use to contact us (your phone provider, your email provider) and to the ordinary way we handle business correspondence.

Information collected automatically

This is a static, informational website. At present it does not use analytics or advertising trackers. If that changes in the future, this policy — and the Cookie Policy — will be updated to describe what is collected and why.

Embedded third-party content

The Contact page embeds a Google Maps view to show our office location. Google may set its own cookies or collect usage data when that map loads, governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We also load fonts from Google Fonts, which may involve a request to Google's servers.

How we use information

Any details you send us through the contact form or directly by phone/email are used only to respond to your enquiry, provide a quote, or otherwise assist with your tooling requirement. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Data retention

We retain business correspondence (emails, quote requests) for as long as reasonably necessary for our commercial and legal record-keeping purposes.

Your rights

You may ask us what information we hold about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete it, subject to any legal or accounting obligations we have to retain business records. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example as the website adds new features (such as a contact-form captcha or analytics). The "Last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.
