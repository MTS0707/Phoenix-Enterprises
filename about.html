AUTHORIZED DEALER

Our Principal Brands

TRIMAT

Phoenix Enterprises operates as an authorized channel partner of TRIMAT, one of India's largest manufacturers of solid carbide and indexable cutting tools. TRIMAT brings modern production facilities, the latest coatings and chip-breaker technology, and a full range of solid carbide tooling for diverse machining applications.

DEREK

DEREK is our second principal brand, supplying precision inserts, tool holders and tool-holding systems used across turning, milling and grooving operations. Paired with TRIMAT, it lets Phoenix Enterprises offer customers a genuine second-source comparison on tooling and grade selection for every application.
