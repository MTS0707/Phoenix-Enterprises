Cookie Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, which ones this website currently uses, and the choices available to you. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website can place on your device to remember information — for example, your language preference or whether you've seen a particular notice. Similar technologies include local storage and third-party embeds that may set their own cookies.

Cookies this website sets directly

This website does not set its own first-party cookies. It is a static site with no login, shopping cart, or user accounts, so there's currently nothing on our side that needs to remember who you are between visits.

The one exception is the cookie-consent banner itself: when you click Accept or Reject, we remember that choice using your browser's local storage (not a cookie) so the banner doesn't reappear on every page. This is stored only on your device and is never sent to us or any third party.

Cookies set by embedded third-party content

Some elements on this site are loaded from third-party services, which may set their own cookies or use similar tracking technology independent of us:

  • Google Maps — embedded on the Contact page to show our office location. Google may set cookies when this loads; see Google's Cookie Policy.
  • Google Fonts — used for the site's typography. Loading a font from Google's servers may involve a request that includes standard technical information (like your IP address), per Google's own policies.

We do not currently use Google Analytics, advertising pixels, or any other tracking or marketing cookies on this site.

Managing cookies

Most browsers let you view, block, or delete cookies through their settings. Since this website does not rely on first-party cookies for any core functionality, blocking cookies should not affect your ability to browse it — though it may affect how embedded content like Google Maps behaves.

Future changes

If we add features that require cookies in the future — for example, a captcha on the contact form or website analytics — we will update this policy to describe what's added and why. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
